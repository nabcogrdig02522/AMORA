# Founder Launch Checklist

## Product and governance
- [ ] Confirm brand ownership, domain, company entity and app-store account ownership.
- [ ] Approve MVP scope, plan prices, free/paid feature matrix and refund policy.
- [ ] Approve community standards, moderation playbooks, appeals and escalation contacts.
- [ ] Obtain Ghana legal/privacy review (including Data Protection Act 2012, Act 843), cross-border data transfer advice and regulator registration/filings as applicable.
- [ ] Publish privacy notice, terms, cookie/SDK disclosures, retention schedule and contact channel.

## Safety and eligibility
- [ ] Establish robust 18+ age assurance and underage incident process.
- [ ] Define consent and retention for selfies/identity evidence; appoint authorised reviewers.
- [ ] Define response SLAs for threats, stalking, fraud, impersonation and urgent reports.
- [ ] Ensure block/report/unmatch work end-to-end; test moderation staffing and escalation.
- [ ] Provide account access, export, correction and deletion request processes.

## Payments and finance
- [ ] Verify Paystack merchant approval, settlement account and test/live webhook configuration.
- [ ] Sign off prices and tax/accounting treatment; reconcile minor-unit precision by currency.
- [ ] Publish renewal, cancellation, refund and failed-payment terms.
- [ ] Establish daily reconciliation and chargeback handling.

## Technical operations
- [ ] Staging acceptance tests pass; no unresolved P0/P1 issues.
- [ ] RLS and penetration/security review complete.
- [ ] Monitoring, alerting, backups, restore test and incident runbook complete.
- [ ] Support and moderation team trained; escalation roster on duty.
- [ ] App signing, privacy labels, store screenshots, age rating and review notes complete.
- [ ] Closed pilot feedback reviewed and release decision signed.

## Regional rollout
- [ ] Ghana pilot metrics and safety outcomes reviewed.
- [ ] Each next country has approved local terms/privacy, payment provider, settlement and customer support.
- [ ] Country-specific currency formatting, taxes, consumer rules, language and data-transfer controls validated.
- [ ] Country market flag remains off until country owner signs readiness.
